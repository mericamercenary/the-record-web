# U.S. Constitution: 19th Amendment: women's right to vote

> Read-only record from THE RECORD; cite sources; no verdicts.

- Kind: Constitution text
- Page: https://mericamercenary.github.io/the-record-web/ctx/constitution/amd19.md
- Last updated: Fixed text

The right of citizens of the United States to vote shall not be denied or abridged by the United States or by any State on account of sex.

Congress shall have power to enforce this article by appropriate legislation.
