# U.S. Constitution: 27th Amendment: pay raises for Congress wait for an election

> Read-only record from THE RECORD; cite sources; no verdicts.

- Kind: Constitution text
- Page: https://mericamercenary.github.io/the-record-web/ctx/constitution/amd27.md
- Last updated: Fixed text

No law, varying the compensation for the services of the Senators and Representatives, shall take effect, until an election of Representatives shall have intervened.
