# U.S. Constitution: Article I, Section 8 (taxing and spending): power to tax and spend for the general welfare

> Read-only record from THE RECORD; cite sources; no verdicts.

- Kind: Constitution text
- Page: https://mericamercenary.github.io/the-record-web/ctx/constitution/art1-s8-c1.md
- Last updated: Fixed text

The Congress shall have Power To lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for the common Defence and general Welfare of the United States; but all Duties, Imposts and Excises shall be uniform throughout the United States;
