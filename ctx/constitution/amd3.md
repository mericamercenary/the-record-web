# U.S. Constitution: 3rd Amendment: no housing soldiers in homes

> Read-only record from THE RECORD; cite sources; no verdicts.

- Kind: Constitution text
- Page: https://mericamercenary.github.io/the-record-web/ctx/constitution/amd3.md
- Last updated: Fixed text

No Soldier shall, in time of peace be quartered in any house, without the consent of the Owner, nor in time of war, but in a manner to be prescribed by law.
