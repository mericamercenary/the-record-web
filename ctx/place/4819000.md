## Plain summary

Dallas is a city in Collin, Dallas, Denton, Kaufman, and Rockwall counties in Texas. It covers about 340 square miles of land and 44 square miles of water, and about 1,329,491 people live there (Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025).
