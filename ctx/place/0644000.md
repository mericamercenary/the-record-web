## Plain summary

Los Angeles is a city in Los Angeles County in southern California. It covers about 471 square miles of land and 31 square miles of water, and about 3,869,089 people live there (Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025).

## Local government

Los Angeles has a mayor and a 15-member City Council. Voters elect the mayor, the city attorney, and the controller citywide, and one councilmember in each of 15 districts. (source: https://clerk.lacity.gov/articles/current-elected-officials)
