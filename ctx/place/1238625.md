## Plain summary

Lake Placid is a town in Highlands County in Florida. It covers about 4.7 square miles of land and 0.1 square miles of water, and about 2,599 people live there (Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025).

## Local government

Lake Placid has a mayor/council form of government: voters elect a mayor and four Town Council members to four-year terms, and the council picks one member as vice mayor each year. (source: https://www.lakeplacidfl.net/201/Elected-Officials)
