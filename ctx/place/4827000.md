## Plain summary

Fort Worth is a city in Denton, Johnson, Parker, Tarrant, and Wise counties in Texas. It covers about 352 square miles of land and 8.2 square miles of water, and about 1,028,117 people live there (Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025).
